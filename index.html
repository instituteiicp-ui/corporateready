01 — The Problem

Anyone can share a link. Very few can deliver completion.

The bottleneck is not access to platforms. Every student already has the same links, the same resume, and the same lack of proof. The bottleneck is execution.

Government data proves the gap: the National Career Service portal posted 1.94 crore vacancies but recorded only 35.23 lakh actual placements. If a government portal cannot convert access into outcomes at scale, a forwarded link definitely will not.

04 — The Honesty Slide

We will never sell you a placement number. Here's why that protects you.

No honest partner can guarantee a job — not even a full degree does. Any provider promising 100% placement is selling you a risk you will pay for later. We guarantee the things we control, report them monthly, and let the results speak.

We guarantee in writing
  • Every eligible student completes a live, ATS-optimised profile on NCS, AICTE and one private platform
  • 3–5 verified application opportunities per eligible student to live, interview-bearing drives, walk-ins, job fairs and hiring challenges
  • 2 documented mock interviews per student with written feedback
  • A QR-verified Virtual Project Experience Certificate — self-completed project, clearly labelled, no employer name
  • A monthly readiness report you can show parents and management
We do not guarantee
  • A job offer
  • A specific number of final selections
  • A specific salary package
  • A named employer on the certificate
  • A placement percentage

Student data, consent, and no-logo policy. Interview Champ runs entirely in the student's browser — answers and results never leave the device. Student data used for any employer-facing module is processed only on the institute's written authorisation. The institute remains the data owner. Corporate Ready will never display a company logo or name unless that company has actually hired a student through the institute's own placement efforts.

Start with a 30-day pilot on one batch. Judge us on the report.

One batch. Thirty students. Thirty days. Written metrics.

If the readiness report does not meet the agreed metrics, you do not continue and you do not pay the balance. No placement-percentage clause. No MoU trap.

We will not sign a placement-percentage clause. Not in a pilot, not in an MoU. Government schemes carry penal provisions; private MoUs become disputes. Our written guarantee is process, not outcome — and that is exactly why it is enforceable.

Eligibility rule: Pilot metrics are measured on eligible students — those who attend onboarding and respond to at least one outreach within 7 days. Students who do not respond are reported separately and excluded from the percentage calculations. This is fair to both sides and standard practice for any training programme.
